{%- comment -%} An ordinary video in a text — with its sound and the browser's controls, it plays only when the reader presses play. (A short silent loop standing in for a GIF is clip.html.) preload="none" means nothing but the poster downloads until then, and playsinline keeps it in the page on an iPhone instead of jumping to full screen. Like every video in a text it fills the column. include.src the video file (.mp4) include.poster still frame shown until it plays include.width pixel size of the video — with height it reserves the space, include.height so the page does not jump when the poster arrives include.alt what the video shows {%- endcomment -%}
