Visitors who ask for reduced motion get the poster and a play button instead. include.src the .mp4 include.poster still frame shown until it plays include.width pixel size of the clip — with height it reserves the space, include.height so the page does not jump when the poster arrives include.alt what the clip shows {%- endcomment -%}
