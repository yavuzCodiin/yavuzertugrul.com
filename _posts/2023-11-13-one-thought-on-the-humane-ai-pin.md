Technology has profoundly reshaped our lives, transforming from a luxury of the elite to a personal companion nestled within a pin. Gone are the days when data traveled by plane; now, an AI assistant sits discreetly on our lapel. From Steve Jobs’ vision of personal computing to the terabytes in our palms — where videos stream and music plays at a tap — we’ve moved beyond mere computing to an era of living with intelligence woven into the very fabric of our daily wear.

Imran Chaudhri and Bethany Bongiorno are not just former Apple luminaries; they are the visionaries who helped sculpt the digital era. Chaudhri, with a two-decade tenure at Apple, has influenced our digital interactions profoundly, having a hand in everything from the Macintosh to the iPhone. His commitment to human-centric design is evident in the [thousands of patents](http://imranchaudhri.com/) bearing his name. Bongiorno, with her command over software project management, was instrumental in launching groundbreaking products like the iPad.

Together, they form the nucleus of Hu.Ma.Ne — a company that looks beyond today’s technological landscape to envision a more personal and intelligent future. They remind us that at the heart of every byte and pixel is the human experience, a sentiment echoing Steve Jobs’ own philosophy. With the Hu.Ma.Ne AI Pin, they aim to bring back an honest human connection in the age of computing, reshaping not just how we interact with technology, but how it interacts with us, embedding the essence of personal care and intelligence into our daily lives.

The AI Pin comes in three colors which are Eclipse, Equinox, Lunar and comprises two parts: the computer and the battery. It operates independently of smartphones, though it requires a $24 monthly internet access fee through a partnership with T-Mobile. The AI Pin is powered by a Qualcomm Snapdragon processor and includes an LED notification light, camera, speaker, and various sensors. It can perform photo and video recording with a tap, and the content can be accessed via Hu.Ma.Ne’s web application. The AI Pin’s software is powered by Hu.Ma.Ne’s proprietary AI, which understands user commands and queries, acting as a personal AI assistant. Priced at $699, it will be available for order in the U.S. starting November 16
